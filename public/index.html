udienz.web.id sign-in

This is the sign-in service for the apps at udienz.web.id. It lets you sign in to those apps with your Google account, so each app does not have to handle passwords itself. Signing in is optional in every app.

Apps that use it

  • QuranWeb: a free Qur'an reading site (Arabic text, Indonesian translation, recitation audio and tafsir).

What it receives

From Google, only your name, email address and a Google account identifier. It does not read your mail, files or contacts. Details are in the privacy policy (Bahasa Indonesia and English).
